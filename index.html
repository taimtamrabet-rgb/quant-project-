How This Works

No quizzes
There are no trivia questions to get "right." Every screen is a real-life choice with trade-offs — you decide what your character does.
Stats
Intelligence, Charisma, Discipline, Health, and Happiness (0–20) build up from how you spend your years and shape what jobs and outcomes you get.
Skill
A 0–100 job-performance meter, separate from your stats. It rises when you take on hard coding projects and grind, and drives raises and promotions.
Coding tasks
A handful of moments — a class project, a production bug, your own trading signal — hand you a real Python editor running right in your browser. Tests grade the code itself, not a multiple-choice guess.
Founding a fund
Once you've got savings and a track record, you can leave the desk and raise outside capital. Fees follow the real "2 and 20" hedge-fund model.
