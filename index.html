Trading Floor Glossary

Percent change
How much a price went up or down, as a fraction of the starting price, times 100.
Ratio
A comparison of two numbers, like 3:2 — for every 3 of one thing, there are 2 of another.
Probability
How likely something is, written as a fraction: favorable outcomes ÷ total outcomes.
Simple interest
Money earned on savings: Interest = Principal × rate × time.
Mean (average)
Add up all the values, then divide by how many there are.
Portfolio
All the investments a trader owns, added together.
